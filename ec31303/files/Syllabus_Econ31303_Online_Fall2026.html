Course Description:

As a continuation of Principles of Macro, this course develops the tools of modern macroeconomics and applications at an intermediate level. The course covers the analysis of such economic indicators as aggregate output, unemployment and inflation (or deflation) in different time horizons. It also discusses the views and controversies of different schools of thought concerning the effectiveness of government policies in altering these aggregates.

Value of the Course:

The course will provide students with a deeper understanding of macroeconomic concepts critical to informed decision making, whether as a consumer, businessperson, voter, or policymaker.

Emergency Procedures:

Many types of emergencies can occur on campus; instructions for specific emergencies such as severe weather, active shooter, or fire can be found at http://emergency.uark.edu/. Two examples of emergencies and recommended procedures are:

  • Severe Weather (e.g.,tornado warning):

  • Follow the directions of the instructor or emergency personnel.

  • Seek shelter in the basement or interior room or hallway on the lowest floor, putting as many walls as possible between you and the outside.

  • If you are in a multi-story building, and you cannot get to the lowest floor, pick a hallway in the center of the building.

  • Stay in the center of the room, away from exterior walls, windows, and doors.

  • Violence/Active Shooter–use “CADD”:

    • CALL – 9-1-1.
    • AVOID – If possible, self-evacuate to a safe area outside the building. Follow directions of police officers.
    • DENY – Barricade the door with desk, chairs, bookcases or any items. Move to a place inside the room where you are not visible. Turn off the lights and remain quiet. Remain there until told by police it’s safe.
    • DEFEND – Use chairs, desks, cell phones or whatever is immediately available to distract and/or defend yourself and others from attack.
