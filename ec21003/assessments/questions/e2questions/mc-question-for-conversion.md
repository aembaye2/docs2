1.	GDP is an imperfect measure of a country’s well-being because it does not capture 
a.	The amount of government involvement in the economy 
b.	How much leisure time a country takes. 
c.	How much a country spends on goods. 
d.	GDP is a perfect measure of well-being.  
Answer: B

2.	Which of the following best describes inflation?
a.	Severe, negative decline in the level of prices in an entire economy.
b.	An outburst of high changes in the level of prices in an entire economy.
c.	A unit-free number derived from the price level over a number of years.
d.	A general and ongoing rise in the level of prices in an entire economy.
Answer: D

3.	Which of the following best describes Potential GDP?
a.	positive short run relationship between the price level for output and real GDP, holding the prices of inputs fixed.
b.	The amount of total spending on domestic goods and services in an economy.
c.	The maximum quantity that an economy can produce given full employment of its existing levels of labor, physical capital, technology, and institutions.
d.	The total quantity of output firms will produce and sell.
Answer: C

4. While the sale price of old car by a dealer is not part of GDP, the dealer’s commission is

a. True
b. False
c. Uncertain

5. Which of the following would NOT be included in the calculation of GDP for 2026?

8. Which of the following correctly represents the relationship between GNP and GDP?

A. GNP = GDP − Citizens’ income earned abroad + Foreigners’ income earned domestically

9. An increase in budget deficit shifts __________

a. the supply of loanable funds to the right, decreasing interest rates and increasing investment
b. the supply of loanable funds to the left, increasing interest rates and decreasing investment
c. the demand for loanable funds to the right, decreasing interest rates and increasing investment
